Snowflakes fell softly over the sleeping village, blanketing the cobblestone streets in a thick layer of pristine, undisturbed white powder tonight. Glowing lanterns hung outside each doorway, offering a warm invitation to those still wandering in the bitter cold winter air. Peace settled over everything. A gentle breeze whispered through the ancient cedar trees, carrying the scent of pine and wild honeysuckle. The quiet rhythm of the river provided a soothing backdrop to the peaceful evening. Children laughed as they prepared for the morning, dreaming of sleds and hills, while the village fires burned low in hearths.
