Bright sapphire waters lapped against the weathered wooden docks, where small fishing boats bobbed rhythmically in the harbor. Distant seagulls cried out as they circled the horizon, searching for their final meal of the day. The salty air felt cool against the skin as the temperature dropped. A gentle breeze whispered through the ancient cedar trees, carrying the scent of pine and wild honeysuckle. The quiet rhythm of the river provided a soothing backdrop to the peaceful evening. Fishermen began to secure their nets and prepare for the long journey back to the shore, leaving the deep ocean behind.
