Dark shadows crept along the stone corridors of the castle, where flickering torches cast dancing light upon the ancient, moss-covered walls. Silence reigned supreme, broken only by the distant echo of footsteps on the cold, hard floor. Memories of a lost kingdom remained. A gentle breeze whispered through the ancient cedar trees, carrying the scent of pine and wild honeysuckle. The quiet rhythm of the river provided a soothing backdrop to the peaceful evening. A silver moon peeked through the high stained-glass windows, illuminating the forgotten relics that lay abandoned in the grand hall, waiting for discovery.
