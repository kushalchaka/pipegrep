Jagged cliffs rose sharply from the churning sea, where waves crashed against the rocks with a ferocity that demanded total respect. The smell of brine was thick in the air, mixing with the harsh spray of the turbulent Atlantic. Time seemed frozen here. A gentle breeze whispered through the ancient cedar trees, carrying the scent of pine and wild honeysuckle. The quiet rhythm of the river provided a soothing backdrop to the peaceful evening. A sturdy lighthouse stood as a silent guardian, its steady beam sweeping across the darkness, warning sailors away from the treacherous edges of land.
