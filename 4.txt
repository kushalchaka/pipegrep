Heavy rain hammered against the tin roof, creating a thunderous melody that echoed throughout the small, cozy cabin in the woods. Bookshelves lined the walls, filled with forgotten stories and dusty maps of lands long since explored by adventurous travelers. The warmth of the fireplace brought comfort. A gentle breeze whispered through the ancient cedar trees, carrying the scent of pine and wild honeysuckle. The quiet rhythm of the river provided a soothing backdrop to the peaceful evening. A steaming mug of dark coffee rested on the table, its aroma filling the room with a sense of calm.
